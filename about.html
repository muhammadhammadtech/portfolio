About Me

Architecting Reliability

I'm a DevSecOps Engineer - and I didn't start out this way.

Scroll to explore

I didn't start out in tech. My first real work was as a creator - video editing and design, freelancing with actual clients who had real deadlines and real expectations.

That's where I learned the things that don't show up on a resume: how to actually listen to what a client needs, how to deliver something reliable even under pressure, and how to communicate clearly when things go wrong.

Somewhere along the way, I got pulled toward the technical side of how things actually run. Not just building things that look good, but building things that stay up.

That curiosity turned into a full shift. I moved into DevOps and DevSecOps: AWS, Kubernetes, CI/CD, security-first infrastructure. It felt less like starting over and more like finally finding the right tools for the same instinct - build something people can depend on.

Today, I design and ship production-grade infrastructure for SaaS startups. The medium changed. What I actually care about didn't.
